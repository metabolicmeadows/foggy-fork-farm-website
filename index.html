We are a small family farm taking pride in raising livestock naturally. Our registered Nubian and Miniature Nubian dairy goats eat a diet of pasture, fodder, sprouts, and haylage — and with the incredible milk they give us, we make soaps, lotions, and our family's supply of cheese and butter.

We have three children learning where food comes from, how to raise it, and how to love all of God's creations. Our goats are truly a family affair — raised and loved by all. We take great joy in offering family-friendly milkers and kids to customers who share our values.

Our pledge

RAISED
WITHOUT
SHORTCUTS.

No corner-cutting on feed. No unnecessary chemicals. Our herd is managed with fresh garlic, ginger root, and a farm-mixed herbal blend tuned over months of weekly fecal tests and careful observation. We top-dress feed with whole flax, sesame, and chia seeds sourced locally — because what goes in shapes everything that comes out.
